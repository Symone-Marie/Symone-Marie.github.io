SIEM Ops

I'm Symone-Marie Priester. Cybersecurity analyst working in vulnerability management, threat hunting, and SIEM operations. I build real defenses against real attacker behavior and prove they hold under pressure.

My path into security came from a habit of asking why things break. I work on the technical side of the field because that's where the questions live. Vulnerability scans tell a story. Logs tell a story. The job is reading them, knowing what to listen for, and acting on what you find before someone else does.
"The defense matters when you understand what you're defending against. Study the attack first."
At Log(N) Pacific I run vulnerability scans across Windows and Linux environments using Tenable, prioritize by CVSS and operational context, and write PowerShell to remediate at scale. I hunt for IoCs in Defender for Endpoint, looking for brute force, exfiltration patterns, and ransomware behavior, and write KQL queries in Microsoft Sentinel to surface the signal in the noise.
Currently in progress: B.S. Cybersecurity and Information Assurance at WGU (graduating July 2026). CompTIA Security+ scheduled for July 2026. M.S. Cybersecurity and Information Assurance starting August 2026. Active threat hunting and detection rule development on the Cyber Range.
